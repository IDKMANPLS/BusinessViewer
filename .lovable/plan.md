# Franco's Mechanical HVAC Rebrand

## Goal
Rebrand the existing bilingual contractor website into a professional, conversion-focused HVAC site for Franco's Mechanical in Windsor, California. Preserve the established polished layout and interactions while replacing all paving-specific content, photography, trust claims, and search metadata.

## What will change
- Replace the brand, owner, address, phone, service area, and licensing details with the verified Franco's Mechanical information.
- Replace all six paving services with mini-split installation, air conditioning, heating, HVAC repair, thermostats/zoning, and preventive maintenance.
- Rewrite every service detail page with practical system explanations, diagnostic/installation process, pricing factors, maintenance guidance, and post-installation support.
- Replace paving imagery throughout the homepage, gallery, about page, and service pages with a cohesive set of professional HVAC installation and service photography.
- Reframe the gallery as installation and service expertise rather than aesthetic before/after results. Captions will describe the type of work shown without claiming the generated scenes are specific completed Franco's projects.
- Rewrite the homepage around expert installation, responsive service, transparent recommendations, communication, and follow-through after the job.
- Add dedicated system guidance and seasonal comfort tips to the homepage and detailed HVAC FAQs to the services experience.
- Add a service-area map section centered on Windsor and covering nearby Sonoma County communities.
- Replace unsupported BBB, EPA, manufacturer, warranty-length, rating, and insurance claims. Use verified CSLB C-20 license #962011 and its stated active/clear status; describe warranty handling without inventing terms.
- Remove the existing Jim's Paving reviews. Keep the review section ready for the genuine Franco's Mechanical reviews the user will provide, without publishing placeholder testimonials.
- Keep the full English/Spanish experience and update all interface, form, service, FAQ, and location copy in both languages.
- Update every public page's title, description, Open Graph metadata, canonical URL, structured data, sitemap entries, favicon, image alt text, and contact wording for Windsor HVAC searches.
- Update the estimate form fields for HVAC needs. The form will keep its current on-page behavior unless a real delivery inbox/service is supplied.
